# Blue Ridge Outfitters — Company and Example Guide

## Purpose

This guide gives all course examples one consistent world. It is not meant to simulate a complete business. Keep examples simple enough that students can focus on Python and analytics.

## Company

**Blue Ridge Outfitters** is a fictional outdoor retailer with physical stores and an online sales channel.

## Example design rules

1. Prefer business questions over abstract arithmetic.
2. Keep examples realistic but small.
3. Reuse familiar product and store names across the week.
4. Increase complexity gradually.
5. Do not make examples depend on retail expertise.
6. Avoid contrived values that obscure the programming idea.
7. Use the API as external weather data connected through store locations.
8. Use CSV and Excel files for controlled historical business data.

## Progression examples

### Variables

Use product name, price, quantity, discount, and subtotal.

### Conditionals

Use loyalty discounts, free-shipping thresholds, returns, or stock warnings.

### Loops

Use daily sales, shopping-cart items, or store totals.

### Functions

Use reusable order totals, margin estimates, or shipping calculations.

### Strings and collections

Use product names, customer records, orders, and nested weather responses.

### NumPy

Use quantities, prices, daily revenue, discounts, and masks.

### pandas

Use complete order, customer, product, and store tables.

### Capstone

Use merged sales and forecast data to answer practical operational questions.
